Terms of Service

Terms, in plain words.

No legal fog. This is how working with AgentX works: what you pay, when you pay it, what you own, and what happens if plans change. If anything is unclear, ask us before you sign anything.

01Acceptance and quotes

Every quote we send is valid for 30 days. When you accept a quote and pay the starting deposit, you are accepting these terms for that project. If anything in a quote ever conflicts with these terms, the quote you signed off on wins for that project.

02Payment schedule

Projects are billed in milestones: 50% to start, 25% after the development stage, and 25% before release. Monthly plans (SEO, voice agents, subscriptions, care and update plans) are billed monthly in advance. Work starts once the relevant payment has cleared.

03Late payment

If a payment is late, we may pause work and deliveries until it is made. Monthly plans that stay unpaid are paused at the end of the paid period. We will always tell you before anything is paused, nobody likes surprises.

04Your content and delays

Some projects need things from you: text, images, logins, approvals. If those are delayed, the timeline moves by the same amount. If we do not hear from you for a long stretch, we may close the project and bill for the work completed.

05Timelines and acceptance

Your quote includes an estimated timeline, and we work to it. When we deliver work, you have 10 days to review it and tell us about anything that does not match the agreed scope. If we do not hear back within those 10 days, the deliverable is treated as accepted so the project can move forward.

06Revisions and scope changes

Every plan includes the revision rounds listed in it. Changes inside the agreed scope are covered by those rounds. Work outside the scope is quoted separately, in writing, before we do it. We never add work to your bill without your approval.

07Ownership and portfolio

When a project is paid in full, you own the final deliverables: your website, your logo files, your content. Until then, the work remains ours. We may show finished work in our portfolio and case studies; if you need a project kept private, tell us in writing and we will respect that.

08Refunds and cancellation

Either side can cancel in writing. Work completed up to cancellation is payable, and milestone payments cover the work booked in that stage. Monthly services and third-party costs are not refundable once that period has started. If we ever cannot deliver what we agreed, we will tell you early and refund any payment for work not done.

09Third-party costs

Some projects use third-party services: domains, hosting, software tools, platforms, providers, or Meta's charges for WhatsApp and ads. Those costs are yours and are paid by you directly to the provider. In our standard plans there are none beyond the plan price; in custom projects, every expected third-party cost is written into your quote before work starts.

10Care, update and retainer plans

Care plans (websites), update plans (chatbots) and care retainers (automations) are optional monthly services that cover the updates, checks and small changes described in each plan. They are billed monthly in advance and can be cancelled anytime, taking effect at the end of the paid month. Your website, chatbot or automation keeps working without them.

11Liability

We are responsible for the work we agree to deliver, and we will fix anything that does not match the agreed scope. Our total liability for a project is limited to the fees you paid us for that project. We are not liable for third-party platforms, providers or services, or for losses that follow from them.
