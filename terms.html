Terms of Service

Terms, in plain words.

This page explains how working with us works. What you pay and when, what you own, what happens if plans change. We kept it short and readable. If something is still unclear, just ask.

01Acceptance and quotes

Every quote we send is valid for 30 days. Accepting a quote and paying the deposit means you accept these terms for that project. If a quote ever says something different from these terms, the quote wins for that project.

02Payment schedule

Projects are billed in milestones: 50% to start, 25% after the development stage, 25% before release. Monthly plans (SEO, voice agents, subscriptions, care and update plans) are billed monthly in advance. Work starts when the payment clears.

03Late payment

If a payment is late, we can pause work and deliveries until it's made. Monthly plans that stay unpaid are paused at the end of the paid period. We'll always warn you first. Nobody likes surprises.

04Your content and delays

Some projects need things from you: text, images, logins, approvals. If those arrive late, the timeline moves by the same amount. If we don't hear from you for a long stretch, we may close the project and bill for the work done so far.

05Timelines and acceptance

Your quote includes an estimated timeline and we work to it. When we deliver work, you get 10 days to review it and flag anything that doesn't match what we agreed. If we hear nothing in those 10 days, the work counts as accepted and the project moves on.

06Revisions and scope changes

Every plan includes the revision rounds listed in it. Changes inside the agreed scope are covered by those rounds. Work outside the scope gets quoted separately, in writing, before we do it. We never add work to your bill without your say-so.

07Ownership and portfolio

When a project is paid in full, the final deliverables are yours: your website, your logo files, your content. Until then the work stays ours. We may show finished work in our portfolio. If you need a project kept private, tell us in writing and we will respect that.

08Refunds and cancellation

Either side can cancel in writing. Work completed up to that point is payable, and milestone payments cover the stage they were booked for. Monthly services and third-party costs can't be refunded once that period has started. And if we ever can't deliver what we agreed, we'll tell you early and refund whatever was paid for work not done.

09Third-party costs

Some projects use third-party services: domains, hosting, software, platforms, providers, or Meta's charges for WhatsApp and ads. Those costs are yours, paid by you straight to the provider. Our standard plans have none beyond the plan price. In custom projects, every expected third-party cost is written into your quote before work starts.

10Care, update and retainer plans

Care plans, update plans and care retainers are optional monthly services. They cover the updates, checks and small changes described in each plan. They're billed monthly in advance and you can cancel anytime; the cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month. Your website, chatbot or automation keeps working without them.

11Liability

We're responsible for the work we agree to deliver, and we'll fix anything that doesn't match the agreed scope. Our total liability on a project is limited to the fees you paid us for that project. We're not liable for third-party platforms, providers or services, or for losses that follow from them.

12Questions about these terms

If anything here is unclear, ask us before you sign anything. Email info@agentxwebsolutions.com or WhatsApp +92 311 3013137 and we'll explain it in plain words.
