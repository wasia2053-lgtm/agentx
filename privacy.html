Privacy Policy

Your information, handled straight.

What we collect when you contact us, why we collect it, which services process it, and how to get it corrected or deleted. In plain words, like everything else on this site.

01What we collect

When you contact us through this website, we receive what you send us: your name, email address, company name and your message, through our contact form. When you book a call, we receive your name, email and anything you add to the booking. When you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we receive your number or address and the conversation. Our website also uses Google Analytics, which collects anonymous usage data (pages visited, device type, approximate location) so we can understand how the site is used.

02How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, prepare quotes, run your project, and provide the services you asked for. We may use anonymised analytics to improve the website. We do not sell your information, and we do not use your contact details for unrelated marketing. If you become a client, we keep project communication and records for as long as the work and normal business record-keeping require.

03The services that process it

Some tools process information on our behalf: Formspree delivers our contact form messages to our inbox, Calendly handles call bookings, Google Analytics measures website usage, and WhatsApp and email providers carry our conversations. Each of them has its own privacy policy, and information sent through them is handled under their terms as well as ours.

04Cookies

This website does not set advertising cookies of its own. Google Analytics may set cookies to measure visits. The services we link to, such as Calendly, may set their own cookies when you use them.

05How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are kept only as long as we reasonably need them for follow-up, then deleted. Client project records are kept while we work together and for a reasonable period afterwards for business and legal record-keeping.

06Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email wasia2053@gmail.com and we will handle it. If you no longer want to hear from us, one message is enough; we will stop.

07Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page. This version was published in October 2026.
