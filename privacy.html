Privacy Policy

Your information, handled straight.

What we collect when you get in touch, why we collect it, and who else touches it along the way. Plus how to get your details corrected or deleted.

01What we collect

When you contact us through this website, we get what you send: your name, email, company name and your message. Book a call and we get your name, email and anything you add to the booking. Message us on WhatsApp or by email and we get your number or address and the conversation itself. We also use Google Analytics. It collects anonymous usage data (pages visited, device type, rough location) that helps us see how the site is being used.

02How we use it

We use your details to reply to you, prepare quotes, run your project and deliver the work you asked for. Anonymous analytics help us improve the website. We don't sell your information. We don't use your contact details for unrelated marketing. If you become a client, we keep project messages and records for as long as the work and normal business record-keeping need them.

03The services that process it

A few tools handle information for us. Formspree delivers our contact form messages to our inbox. Calendly handles call bookings. Google Analytics measures website visits. WhatsApp and email providers carry our conversations. Each one has its own privacy policy, so information sent through them is covered by their terms too.

04Cookies

This website doesn't set advertising cookies of its own. Google Analytics may set cookies to measure visits. Services we link to, like Calendly, may set their own cookies when you use them.

05How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't turn into projects are kept only as long as we need them for follow-up, then deleted. Client records stay with us while we work together, and for a reasonable time afterwards for business and legal records.

06Your choices

You can ask at any time what we hold about you. Ask us to correct it or delete it and we will. Email info@agentxwebsolutions.com. And if you don't want to hear from us again, one message is enough. We'll stop.

07Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page. This version was published in October 2026.
